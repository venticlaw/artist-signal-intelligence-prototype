Investor story

The story is not a big artist database. The story is measurable discovery coverage.

ASI is designed to prove that a painful manual A&R workflow can become a repeatable intelligence funnel: target lanes, approved source rows, scored candidates, human review, watchlist decisions, and paid demand for deeper scouting.

Business value

The product is not another data dashboard. It is a better A&R operating loop.

01

Find earlier

Spot artists, scenes, and creator networks before momentum is obvious across every traditional industry surface.

02

Review faster

Replace scattered screenshots and manual research with one source-labeled prospect brief, scoring view, and team notes layer.

03

Learn from passes

Capture why A&R teams pass, watch, shortlist, or request more context so future recommendations improve from human judgment.

04

Monetize insight

Package watchlists, paid scouting reports, custom discovery packets, and done-for-you market intelligence around repeatable signal workflows.

Workflow

From target lanes to boardroom-ready artist intelligence.

  1. Target

    Define genre lanes, comparable artists, territories, buyer priorities, and source policy.

  2. Search

    Run approved daily discovery routes across public signals and provider exports.

  3. Score

    Separate momentum, audience quality, network context, confidence, and commercial fit.

  4. Review

    Give A&R a clean queue with evidence, notes, pass reasons, and next-action state.

  5. Learn

    Turn human feedback into the next day of sharper target expansion and search logic.

Why now

Artist discovery is moving faster than label research workflows.

Short-form video, niche communities, creator collaborations, and micro-scenes can produce valuable artist momentum before legacy charts and databases fully reflect it. The buyer does not need more noise. They need a trusted way to prioritize which signals deserve human attention today.

Initial buyer lanes

  • Distributor artist-development teams
  • Managers scouting complementary roster opportunities
  • Playlist, marketing, and creator-campaign teams
  • Brand and music partnership buyers
