Investor story

The story is not a big artist database. The story is roster accountability.

Earshot is designed to prove that discovery, roster management, and artist investment tracking can live in one familiar workflow: prospects, signed artists, budgets, campaign status, team notes, source-approved signals, and next business actions.

Business value

The product is not another data dashboard. It is a label operating layer.

01

Spot artists, scenes, and creator networks before momentum is obvious across every traditional industry surface.

02

Account for roster value

Track signed artists across deal status, spend, recoupment, campaign readiness, risk, and next business action.

03

Move teams faster

Give A&R, marketing, finance, operations, and partnerships a shared artist view that feels closer to a CRM than a research lab.

04

Monetize insight

Package watchlists, paid scouting reports, custom discovery packets, and done-for-you market intelligence around repeatable signal workflows.

Workflow

Two lanes, one artist operating system.

  1. Prospect

    Define genre lanes, comparable artists, territories, buyer priorities, and source policy.

  2. Discover

    Run approved daily discovery routes across public signals and provider exports.

  3. Roster

    Track signed artists by status, owner, release readiness, campaign state, and business risk.

  4. Invest

    Connect advances, marketing budgets, video spend, recoupment, exposure, and ROI questions.

  5. Act

    Keep decisions, notes, and next steps visible so the whole team knows what happens next.

Why now

Artist businesses move faster than label operating workflows.

Short-form momentum, release cycles, creator collaborations, campaign budgets, and partnership opportunities all move at once. The buyer does not need more noise. They need a trusted way to see which artists need attention, what money is exposed, and who owns the next step.

Initial buyer lanes

  • Distributor artist-development teams
  • Managers scouting complementary roster opportunities
  • Playlist, marketing, and creator-campaign teams
  • Brand and music partnership buyers
  • Finance and operations teams tracking artist investments
